## 3. Core Modules Implemented (20 Phase-1 + 6 Modular Student Hub)

1. **Dual Registration & Profiles:** Separate business & creator media kits with verified audience tags.
2. **Smart Creator Discovery:** Multi-filter by niche, locality, engagement rate %, and budget ceiling. Side-by-side comparison tray.
3. **Hyperlocal Proximity Matching:** Austin campus & downtown neighborhood radius mapping.
4. **Budget-to-Campaign Intelligence:** Algorithmic creator bundle recommendations.
5. **Campaign Marketplace:** 9-state lifecycle: Draft ➔ Published ➔ In Progress ➔ Completed.
6. **Creator Opportunity Score:** Transparent 0–100 matching formula without vanity follower bias.
7. **Collaboration Management Hub:** Central workspace with deliverable checklists, draft links, and 2-tier approval workflow.
8. **Direct Contextual Messaging:** Secure chat thread attached to each collaboration agreement.
9. **Conversion Tracking:** Dynamic in-store QR code generation + unique creator promo codes.
10. **Interactive Simulators:** Click **"Simulate Customer QR Scan"** or **"Simulate Coupon Checkout"** to see live footfall and sales attribution update in real-time.
11. **Campaign ROI Dashboard:** Live Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) & creator-by-creator sales breakdown.
12. **Creator Protection System:** Built-in 2-revision limit, milestone releases, and dispute ticket submission.
13. **Mutual Ratings & Reviews:** Two-way feedback strictly gated to completed collaborations.
14. **Local Creator Discovery Map:** Interactive Leaflet.js neighborhood map with privacy-first pin locations.
15. **AI Content Brief Generator:** Generates Reel hooks, 30-second storyboards, captions, and CTAs.
16. **Modular Student Talent Hub:** Freelance gigs, skill matching, and verified experience certificates with unique verification UUIDs.
17. **Super Admin Panel:** Platform KPI health monitor, feature flags, and dispute resolution queue.
